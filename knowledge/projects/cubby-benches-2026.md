# Summary

Ten backless cubby benches in two widths that form continuous runs and nest
for storage. Shared section: **17 in deep × 18.5 in tall, zero overhang**;
recessed 2×4 base (1.5 in inset) for stacking. 3-ft = 3 cubbies @ 11 in;
4-ft = 4 cubbies @ 11-1/16 in. 3/4-in plywood, butt joints, glue + screws,
no face frame, filled-and-sanded edges (no banding).

# Status

- **Phase 1 — six 3-ft benches: in progress** (July 2026). Materials: 4 sheets
  3/4 ply + 3 new 2×4s + 8 on-hand 54 1/8 offcuts
  (see [materials](/shop/materials-on-hand.md)).
- **Phase 2 — four 4-ft benches: planned.** 4 sheets (one per bench) + 6 new
  2×4s.

# Key numbers

| | 3-ft | 4-ft |
|---|---|---|
| Finished | 36 × 17 × 18.5 | 48 × 17 × 18.5 |
| Cubbies | 3 @ 11 in | 4 @ 11-1/16 in |
| Base (2×4 on edge) | 33 × 14 | 45 × 14 |
| Weight | ~45–50 lb | ~60–65 lb |

Each base = 2 rails (33) + 3 rungs (2 ends + 1 center, all 11), assembled 2×4-on-edge as a ladder, inset 1.5 in all around → 33 × 14 footprint. The 12 rails + 18 rungs come out exactly — no parts left over (new stick 3 has room for 2 extra rungs if you miscut).

WANT TO BUY 2 STICKS INSTEAD OF 3? Cut ten rungs from your long 101½ × 1½ × 1½ 2×2s; then sticks 1 & 2 only supply rails and you skip stick 3. Trade-off: 2×2 rungs sit ~2 in below the shelf, so they no longer back up the shelf mid-span (the sides and dividers still carry seating load from above). Fine for this base — just a small step off the drawing.

Order of operations

Rip & crosscut all plywood; label every piece with tape.
Cut 2×4 base parts; build 6 ladder bases (glue + 3-in screws).
Build 1 bench fully; dry-stack 2 & set side-by-side to confirm fit.
Batch-assemble the remaining 5 carcasses.
Fasten tops from inside; add pads; sand, prime, finish.
Fasten every top with screws from inside — it's the lifting handle. All benches are two-person lifts (~45–50 lb). Don't stack over 3 high unless wall-strapped.
